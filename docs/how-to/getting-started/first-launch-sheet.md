# How to: First Launch Sheet

**Platform:** Electron

## What it is
A guided tour that opens the first time you run TaskWraith. It walks you through signing in to providers, adding a workspace, picking a look, and trying an Ensemble chat.

## Where to find it
It opens by itself on first run. To reopen it later, click the **?** button in the chat-corner rim.

![Top of the First Launch Sheet showing provider status and authentication actions](../images/getting-started__first-launch-sheet.png)

## How to use it
1. Under **1. Sign in to your providers**, check each provider's status and click **Sign in**, **Open Settings**, or **Manage in Settings** on the card. The card text itself is just information.
2. Follow the workspace hint to add your first workspace.
3. Scroll to **5. Choose your starting look** to pick a theme, composer shell, and message-bubble colour. The composer shown there is a preview and does not send anything.
4. Under **8. Try Ensemble chats**, use the **Try this first** card to read or copy the sample task.

## Tips & related
- Copying the sample task never starts a run and never sets seat permissions — set each seat to **Ask** yourself before sending. See [First-run Ensemble task](first-run-ensemble-task.md).
- [Add workspace](add-workspace.md) for setting up your first workspace.
- [Settings entry](../sidebar-navigation/settings-entry.md) to open Settings manually.
