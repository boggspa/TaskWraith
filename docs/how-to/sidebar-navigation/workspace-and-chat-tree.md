# How to: Workspace and Chat Tree

**Platform:** Electron

## What it is
The **Code** surface shows loaded workspaces and their workspace-scoped threads. Linked sub-threads and side chats appear indented beneath their parent. General chats live in **Chat** instead, while **Work** contains Projects that can group top-level chats and threads across either scope.

## Where to find it
In the sidebar's **Code** surface, under **Workspaces**. Switch to **Chat** for General chats or **Work** for the Projects organizer.

![Tight crop of the expanded Test 1 workspace showing a parent thread and its isolated side-chat child](../images/sidebar-navigation__workspace-and-chat-tree.png)

## How to use it
1. Select **Code**, then expand a workspace to see its threads.
2. Look for the indented child row to identify a linked sub-thread or side chat.
3. Click the chevron to collapse a workspace when you need less clutter.
4. To organize a thread independently of its workspace hierarchy, switch to **Work** and add it to a Project.
