# How to: Workspace and Chat Tree

**Platform:** Electron

## What it is
The list under **Code**: each loaded workspace with its chats nested beneath it. Sub-threads and side chats sit indented under the chat that started them. General chats live under **Chat** instead, and **Work** groups chats into Projects across both.

## Where to find it
In the sidebar, under **Code → Workspaces**.

![Tight crop of the expanded Test 1 workspace showing a parent thread and its isolated side-chat child](../images/sidebar-navigation__workspace-and-chat-tree.png)

## How to use it
1. Click **Code**, then click a workspace to expand it and see its chats.
2. Look for an indented row — that is a sub-thread or side chat belonging to the row above it.
3. Click the chevron to collapse a workspace you are done with.
4. To group a chat outside its workspace, switch to **Work** and add it to a Project.
