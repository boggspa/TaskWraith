# How to: Settings and Configuration

**Platform:** Electron

## What it is
Settings is a full-app takeover panel where you configure everything about TaskWraith — app behavior, appearance, AI providers, automation, workspaces, integrations, and data. A sidebar rail groups tabs into six sections, and a search box filters them by name or keyword.

## Where to find it
Click the **Settings** (gear) button in the sidebar footer, or press **Cmd+,** from anywhere in the app. You can also reach it via deep links on the First Launch sheet. Settings replaces the workspace sidebar and main pane until you click **Back to app** or press **Escape**.

## How to use it
1. Open Settings from the sidebar footer gear button or with **Cmd+,**.
2. Use the search box at the top of the Settings sidebar to filter tabs by name or keyword (e.g. "theme", "mcp", "approvals").
3. Pick a tab from one of the six groups — **App**, **AI & Providers**, **Automation**, **Workspaces**, **Integrations**, or **Data** — to configure that area.
4. Click **Back to app** (or press **Escape**) to return to the chat surface.
