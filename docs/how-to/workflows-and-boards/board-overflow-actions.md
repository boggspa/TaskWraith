# How to: Board Overflow Actions

**Platform:** Electron

## What it is
Per-board options available on each workspace board's row in the sidebar: Pin/Unpin, Rename, Duplicate, Archive, and Delete.

## Where to find it
Select **Code**, then in the sidebar's **Workspace Boards** section, hover a board row and click its **⋯** button, or right-click the row.

![Board overflow menu showing pin, rename, duplicate, archive options](../images/workflows-and-boards__board-overflow-actions.png)

## How to use it
1. Select **Code**, open **Workspace Boards** in the sidebar, and locate the board.
2. Click **⋯** on the board's row (or right-click) to open its actions menu.
3. Choose **Pin** (or **Unpin**) to keep the board near the top.
4. Choose **Rename** to edit the board's name.
5. Choose **Duplicate** to create a copy.
6. Choose **Archive** to move the board out of the active list.
7. Choose **Delete** to remove it permanently.

To add a chat to a board, use the chat's overflow menu and select **Add to Workspace Board**. To add a workflow, expand its row in **Workflows** and use **Add to Workspace Board** in its action strip.
