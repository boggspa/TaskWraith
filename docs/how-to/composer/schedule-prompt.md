# How to: Schedule Prompt

**Platform:** Electron

## What it is
Queues the prompt you have written to run later instead of now. It waits and fires on its own at the time you choose.

## Where to find it
On the composer's control row, the icon row under the prompt box, next to the Goal button. Click the clock icon.

![Composer schedule button with quick offset options](../images/composer__schedule-prompt.png)

## How to use it
1. Type your prompt as normal.
2. Click the **clock icon**.
3. Pick a date and time, or tap a quick option — **15m**, **1h**, **Tonight**, or **Tomorrow**.
4. Click **Schedule** to confirm, or **Clear** to drop the time.
5. Send as usual. The prompt is queued instead of dispatched, and the composer clears.

## Tips & related
- A pill above the composer shows the provider, run time, and countdown. Click its **X** to cancel before it fires.
- The clock is disabled in General chats and when no chat is open — scheduling needs a workspace.
- [Goal Button](goal-button.md) — sits next to the clock in the same row.
- [Routines and Scheduled Tasks](../goals-todos-and-scheduling/routines-and-scheduled-tasks.md) — recurring schedules, not just one-offs.
