# How to: Plus Tools Menu

**Platform:** Electron

## What it is
The composer's **+** menu, for adding context to a message — files, folders, a watched app window, Discord messages — plus quick links to workspace tools.

## Where to find it
Click the **+** button at the start of the composer's action row, beside the prompt input.

![Composer + tools menu expanded showing attachments, screen watch, and workspace tools](../images/composer__plus-tools-menu.png)

## How to use it
1. Click **+** to open the menu.
2. Click **Attachments** to add files or images — up to 15 at a time, any type.
3. Click **Folder** to attach a folder the agent can read from. Needs a workspace.
4. Click **Attach app** to watch a running app window, or **Detach app** to stop.
5. Click **Discord context** to pull recent messages from a channel into the chat.

## Tips & related
- The workspace entries at the bottom — **Stats**, **Diff Studio**, **Open Compact Chat**, and **Slash commands** — are hidden in General chats, which have no workspace.
- [Provider, Model, and Permissions Pickers](provider-model-permissions-pickers.md) — the chips beside the **+** button.
- [Slash Commands](slash-commands.md) — the menu that **Slash commands** opens.
- [Inspector Panel](../transcript-and-search/inspector-panel.md) — where **Diff Studio** opens.
